Garden Tips

Simple tips to help your flowers and plants grow healthily and beautifully.

Four important conditions for growing flowers

  • · Watering : Water plants early morning or evening to aviod evaporation.
  • · Sunlight : Most flowers need at least 6-8 hours of sunlight every day.
  • · Pruning : Remove dead leaves and flowers to promote new growth.
  • · Soil : Use nutrient-rich soil or compost for healthy plants.

How to keep flowers fresh longer?

  • · Change water every 2 days
  • · Trim stems at a 45° angle
  • · Keep flowers away from direct sunlight
  • · Remove wilted leaves
